Hollowcrown: classes, trees and the hunt

Four classes, any two of which can be combined. Twelve ascensions. Forty Rites, each with its own specialization tree. One shared Ember Constellation every class draws from. Five league-style mechanics to hunt in the depths.

How it all fits together

Progression per character

  • Level cap 60 for now (raise it as content grows).
  • Every level: 1 Class Point (class skill trees) and 1 Ember Point (shared Constellation).
  • Level 10: choose a second class. You get its skill tree and Rites. Class Points are shared between both trees.
  • Ascension Trials at Depths 5, 10, 15, 20 each give 2 Ascension Points (8 total). You pick one ascension of your first class at the first trial.
  • Rites level up by use (1 to 20). Each level is a point in that Rite's own specialization tree.
  • Specials: each class has an ultimate, charged by its resource. Press LB + RB together (a short chord window stops the flask and slot 6 from also firing).

Defenses and stats

  • Armour (Oathsworn): reduces physical hits; weak against big hits (already built).
  • Evasion (Ranger): chance to avoid a hit entirely; entropy-based so it can't streak.
  • Ember Ward (Magus): a recharging shield on top of life.
  • Spirit Barrier (Shaman): damage shared with your minions and totems.
  • Resistances: fire, cold, lightning, and new chaos (for poison and rift damage). Cap 75%.
  • No attribute points. Gear requirements use level only, so any class can wear anything.
The five Rites already in the game move into classes: Ashen Cleave goes to the Oathsworn; Ember Bolt, Rimeburst, Stormthread and Cinderfall go to the Ember Magus. Existing characters keep working during the transition.

The four classes

Each class has a resource that changes how you play, a Special, a three-branch skill tree and ten Rites. The hero sprite you pick at character creation is your first class.

Dual-class hybrids

At level 10 you add a second class. Your first class sets your look and your ascension; the second adds its tree and Rites.

Ascensions

Three per class. Each has six notables; you earn enough points for four, so no two ascended characters need to look alike.

Rites (skills)

Forty Rites. Six can be on the bar at once (RT, A, X, B, Y, RB). Every Rite levels up as you use it and has its own specialization tree; three worked examples are at the bottom.

Rite specialization trees

Each Rite has about 12 modifier nodes and gains up to 20 points from use. Five Rites can be specialized at once; swapping one out keeps its progress.

The Ember Constellation (shared tree)

One tree of about 150 nodes that every class uses. Each class enters at its own star on the outer ring; dual-classing adds a second entry star. Eight regions, each with a Mastery node where you pick one of three effects, plus keystones that rewrite how you play. Six Ember Sockets hold jewels (Hollow Embers) in a later stage.

Mechanics in the depths

Original twists on the league mechanics you love. Each zone rolls which ones appear; Writs reward you for engaging with them.

Currency and crafting

Shattering items now gives crafting dust instead of nothing. Currencies drop from monsters, Reliquaries, Writs and the Peddler.
